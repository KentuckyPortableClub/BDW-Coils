Designed around portable reality
A coil you can understand, use, and service.

The BDW approach centers on straightforward field hardware and a separate silicone shorting wire. That makes the system easier to work with when you are packing, deploying, changing bands, and tearing down.

For operators who want additional band capability without turning a portable station into a complicated project, that practical mindset is the point.

160 meters
A dedicated low-band configuration.
75 FT

of 26-gauge silicone wire

The 160m configuration removes the whip and uses 75 feet of 26-gauge silicone wire connected to the top of the coil and elevated on a mast. N4BDW reports a field setup using a 40-foot Spiderbeam mast with no tuner and near 1:1 SWR from 1.85–1.89 MHz; a 20-foot mast configuration is also being tested.

Field configuration details are presented as reported in the source material; antenna performance depends on the actual installation and environment.
