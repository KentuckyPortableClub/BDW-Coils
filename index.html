Choose Your Coil

The standard BDW Coil is built for broad portable use. The Buddy Coil keeps the same basic concept in a smaller package for operators who want a compact backpack or case setup.

Full-Size Portable Coil

The BDW Coil

Inspired by the W0BNC design and modified by N4BDW for ease of use and serviceability.

  • Designed for SSB operation
  • Works with 17' and 25' whips
  • 12-inch coil build
  • 40m, 60m, 80m and 160m capability
  • Separate silicone shorting wire
  • Continuity tested before shipment
Compact Field Option

The Buddy Coil

A shorter version for operators who want a small, packable setup.

  • Designed for 30m and 40m
  • Backpack friendly
  • Fits an Apache-style case
  • Works with 17' or 25' whips
  • Silicone jumper lets the whip remain fully extended

Go Lower

Yes — Even 160 Meters.

For the 160m configuration, the whip is removed and a 75-foot length of 26-gauge silicone wire is connected to the top of the coil and elevated on a mast.

N4BDW's reported field setup: 75' of 26-gauge silicone wire elevated to the top of a 40' Spiderbeam mast, with no tuner used. Near 1:1 SWR was reported from 1.85–1.89 MHz. A 20' mast configuration is also being tested.
