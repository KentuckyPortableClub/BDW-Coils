KPC on the iPhone now opens from your PC

This old copy of the app has been retired. The KPC app on your Windows PC hands out the iPhone page itself, and that page is always the same version as the PC. That's the one to use. If you added this page to your Home Screen, delete that icon and add the new address below instead.

At home, on the same Wi-Fi as the PC

  1. On the PC, in the KPC app: STATION → REMOTE LINK → 📡 LET MY PHONE IN. It shows the PC's address and a 6-digit pair code.
  2. On the iPhone, open Safari and type the PC's address with :8731/phone/ on the end, like http://192.168.1.25:8731/phone/
  3. Type the pair code, tap CONNECT TO THE PC, then ✓ APPROVE on the PC.

Talking needs the Tailscale way below. Listening and running the radio work on the plain home address.

Where do I find my PC's address?

A web page on your iPhone can't look inside your home network, so it can't find the PC by itself. The PC shows it to you:

  1. Easiest: in the KPC app on the PC, STATION → REMOTE LINK → 📡 LET MY PHONE IN. The card lists the address (it looks like 192.168.1.25) next to the pair code.
  2. Or in Windows: Start → Settings → Network & internet → Wi-Fi (or Ethernet) → your network's properties. The address is the line called IPv4 address.
  3. Or type it out: press the Windows key, type cmd, open Command Prompt, type ipconfig and press Enter. Use the IPv4 Address line (it usually starts with 192.168 or 10.).

For the Tailscale way, the address is your PC's name in the Tailscale app on the iPhone (tap the PC to copy it), or type tailscale serve status on the PC.

Anywhere, and talking too (Tailscale)

  1. Install Tailscale on the PC and the iPhone and sign in with the same account.
  2. On the PC run tailscale serve --bg 8731 once. It prints your PC's private address.
  3. On the iPhone, open that address with /phone/ on the end, like https://your-pc.your-tailnet.ts.net/phone/
