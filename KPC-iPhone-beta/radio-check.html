2 · Transmit

Press TUNE and watch the radio: ALC should barely move and the watts should be full. ALC high but low power = too much audio, come down. Not enough at 0 dB = turn the radio's USB MOD GAIN up. The radio needs DATA mode with DATA MOD SOURCE = USB.

SEND ONE FT8 CQ transmits once in the next even 15-second slot with your call, like a real contact, so you can see ALC and watts with FT8 tones. It does not answer anyone.
